# Getting Support for LaterBox

Thank you for using LaterBox! We are committed to fostering an active, helpful, and welcoming open-source community.

Here are the best ways to get help, ask questions, report problems, or discuss feature ideas:

---

## 1. 📖 Documentation & Guides

Before filing an issue, please check our documentation knowledge base:

- **Official Documentation**: [docs.laterbox.dev](https://docs.laterbox.dev)
- **User Guide & Shortcuts**: [app.laterbox.dev/tutorial](https://app.laterbox.dev/tutorial)
- **Architecture Overview**: [docs.laterbox.dev/architecture-overview](https://docs.laterbox.dev/architecture-overview)
- **Offline Sync Engine**: [docs.laterbox.dev/local-first-drift-sqlite](https://docs.laterbox.dev/local-first-drift-sqlite)
- **Self-Hosting Guide**: [docs.laterbox.dev/self-hosting-supabase](https://docs.laterbox.dev/self-hosting-supabase)

---

## 2. 💬 Community Q&A & Discussions

For general questions, design feedback, troubleshooting your setup, or sharing workflows:

- **GitHub Discussions**: [github.com/Chaste-Djaziri/laterbox/discussions](https://github.com/Chaste-Djaziri/laterbox/discussions)
  - **Q&A**: Ask how to configure features or resolve errors.
  - **Ideas & RFCs**: Pitch new features or architecture improvements.
  - **Show and Tell**: Share how you use LaterBox in your daily life.

---

## 3. 🐛 Reporting Bugs

If you have encountered a bug or unexpected behavior:

1. Search existing [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Chaste-Djaziri/laterbox/issues) to verify if it has already been reported.
2. If not, open a new [Bug Report](https://github.com/Chaste-Djaziri/laterbox/issues/new?template=bug_report.md) including:
   - Operating system and client version (e.g. macOS 14.2, Web, Android APK).
   - Clear steps to reproduce the issue.
   - Relevant error logs or console outputs.

---

## 4. 💡 Suggesting Features

We welcome ideas that expand LaterBox while preserving our local-first, privacy-focused ethos:

- Open a [Feature Request](https://github.com/Chaste-Djaziri/laterbox/issues/new?template=feature_request.md) describing the problem you want solved and your proposed solution.

---

## 5. 🔒 Security Vulnerabilities

Please **do not** open public issues for security vulnerabilities. Review our [Security Policy](SECURITY.md) and report vulnerabilities confidentially via:

- **GitHub Security Advisory**: [github.com/Chaste-Djaziri/laterbox/security/advisories/new](https://github.com/Chaste-Djaziri/laterbox/security/advisories/new)
- **Email**: `security@laterbox.dev`

---

## 6. 🤝 Contributing

Interested in contributing code, tests, or documentation? Read our [Contributing Guide](CONTRIBUTING.md) to get your local environment running in minutes.
